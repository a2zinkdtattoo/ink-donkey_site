Tattoo artist

Alexey Zamotevsky

Alexey Zamotevsky has been tattooing for 25 years. His foundation is traditional tattooing: confident lines, solid fill and designs drawn from scratch for each client.

Every piece is planned around the body, its placement, size and flow, with an eye on how it will settle into the skin over the years. It starts with a conversation: Alexey listens first, then draws.

How booking works

  1. Send your idea

    Message us on WhatsApp: what you want, where on the body, and roughly how big. References help.

  2. Agree on design and date

    We talk through the idea, settle the size and price, and pick a session date.

  3. Session

    Come rested and fed. Alexey shows you the final sketch before starting.

  4. Healing

    You leave with aftercare instructions. Send a healed photo, we love seeing them.
