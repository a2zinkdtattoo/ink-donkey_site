How booking works

  1. Send your idea

    Message us on WhatsApp: what you want, where on the body, and roughly how big. References help.

  2. Agree on design and date

    We talk through the idea, settle the size and price, and pick a session date.

  3. Session

    Come rested and fed. Alexey shows you the final sketch before starting.

  4. Healing

    You leave with aftercare instructions. Send a healed photo, we love seeing them.
