Original, custom tattoos

About 25 years of tattooing

Alexey Zamotevsky has been tattooing for about 25 years. He has specialized in a range of styles, worked at leading studios in Israel, and keeps learning and growing.

Experience has taught him that no single tattoo suits everyone: every person has a different body, a different character and a unique story.

That is why every tattoo here starts with a conversation and with listening.

A tattoo that tells your story

A memory, love, pain, change, a new beginning, or simply a piece of art you want to wear.

We are here to listen and turn what matters to you into a tattoo that truly feels like yours.

A place that feels like home

First tattoo? A little nervous? That is completely natural. There are no silly questions and no pressure here: ask anything, take a break, take your time.

FAQ

It's my first tattoo. What do I do?

Just message us. We will explain everything in advance, help you choose placement and size, and guide you through every step.

Do I need to book in advance?

It is recommended, so we can give you all the time you need. When there is space, walk-ins are welcome too.

Do you do cover-ups?

Yes. Send a photo of your current tattoo and we will look at the options together, with no judgment.
